# MIXING-FOR-EP

Lead vocal chain presets for the EP. The target sound is a hybrid of **Lil Uzi Vert × Ken Carson × Young Thug**.

## Phases

| Phase | Goal | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Invent 18 lead vocal sound options | ✅ Done: [vocal-chains/phase-1-sound-options.md](vocal-chains/phase-1-sound-options.md) |
| 2 | Lock 5 picks and define spec & scope (28-question interview) | ✅ Done: [vocal-chains/phase-2-spec.md](vocal-chains/phase-2-spec.md) |
| 3 | Build the 5 full vocal chain presets | ✅ Done: [plan](vocal-chains/phase-3-build-plan.md) · [foundation](vocal-chains/presets/00-foundation.md) · [preset sheets](vocal-chains/presets/) |

## The presets

Start with the [foundation](vocal-chains/presets/00-foundation.md): the routing template, the gain standard, and how to read and build a sheet (§5.6–5.7). Then use one preset per song:

| Preset | Sound |
|---|---|
| [02 · Phantom Twin](vocal-chains/presets/02-phantom-twin.md) | A clean, bright lead, shadowed by a dark, distorted twin an octave down at ghost level |
| [04 · Neon Bleach](vocal-chains/presets/04-neon-bleach.md) | Glossy, very bright and hard-tuned, right in your face, over a soft ping-pong bed |
| [11 · Silk Stack](vocal-chains/presets/11-silk-stack.md) | Lush and choir-like: the lead blooms into a 3rd up and an octave down, made from itself |
| [13 · Vampire Haze](vocal-chains/presets/13-vampire-haze.md) | Dark and drenched: a rolled-off top, a dirty hall and near-frozen throws |
| [16 · Rockstar Grit](vocal-chains/presets/16-rockstar-grit.md) | Upfront and gritty: amp-driven snarl, biting mids and a tape slapback |
