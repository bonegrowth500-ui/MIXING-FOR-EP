# Phase 1: 18 Lead Vocal Sound Options

**Lil Uzi Vert × Ken Carson × Young Thug**

Every option below is a lead vocal *sound*: a character, not a chain yet. Pick 5, and each one gets built into a full insert-by-insert vocal chain in the next phase.

Every sound carries all three artists. Only the blend shifts: **5 lean Uzi, 5 lean Ken, 5 lean Thug, and 3 have no single lead artist.**

---

## The DNA we're blending

- **Lil Uzi Vert:** Bright, glossy, hard-tuned melody. Airy, sparkly top end. Stereo delay throws and stacked ad-libs. Ranges from emo haze (*Luv Is Rage 2*) to space-age gloss (*Eternal Atake*) to rock grit (*Pink Tape*).
- **Ken Carson:** Blown-out, clipped, distorted vocals, often buried *inside* the rage synths instead of floating on top. Dark, gothic, chant-like hooks. Lo-fi on purpose and extremely loud (*Project X* → *X* → *A Great Chaos*).
- **Young Thug:** Elastic, warbling, wildly expressive. The voice flips and cracks through the Auto-Tune. Squeals, yelps, croaks and character voices. Warm, lush, heavily layered harmonies and ad-libs (*Barter 6*, *Slime Season*, *Jeffery*, *So Much Fun*).

## How to read each option

- **Blend:** how much of each artist is in the sound, always listed Uzi · Ken · Thug and adding up to 100.
- **Meters** (○○○○○ none → ●●●●● max):
  - **Bright:** top-end shine
  - **Grit:** saturation and distortion
  - **Wet:** reverb and delay
  - **Width:** stereo spread
- **Tune:** how the pitch correction behaves.
  - *Robotic:* instant, with audible note-stepping
  - *Hard:* snaps every note into place
  - *Fast:* quick, but slides still bend
  - *Medium:* smooth and natural
  - *+ humanized:* held notes keep their natural wobble

## I. CROSSBREED: no single artist in charge

### 01 · CERBERUS

`Uzi 34 · Ken 33 · Thug 33` · Tune: **Fast**

Bright ●●●●○ · Grit ●●●○○ · Wet ●●●○○ · Width ●●●●○

**The sound:** The three-headed house sound. A bright, snapping lead with a fuzz engine humming underneath and elastic harmonies wrapped around it. Polished enough for a single, dirty enough for a rage beat. This is the one you default to and tweak from.

**Signature moves**
- Fast tune that snaps to key but still lets slides bend a little
- Main path: fast FET compression → air shelf → de-esser for the gloss

**Hybrid DNA:** Uzi's shine on top · Ken's fire underneath · Thug's bounce and width around the edges.

### 02 · PHANTOM TWIN

`Uzi 40 · Ken 40 · Thug 20` · Tune: **Hard**

Bright ●●●○○ · Grit ●●●○○ · Wet ●●○○○ · Width ●●●○○

**The sound:** Split personality. A clean, bright lead rides on top while a dark, distorted, octave-down "demon twin" shadows every word underneath. Angel and demon in one voice: creepy, hypnotic, hard to forget.

**Signature moves**
- Clean, bright chain on the main vocal
- Parallel demon chain: pitch down an octave (or a heavy formant drop) → distortion → band-limit → tuck it low
- Automate the twin's level so the demon surges forward on key words and punchlines
- Two different rooms: the twin gets a dark short verb, the lead gets a bright delay

**Hybrid DNA:** Uzi = the clean, bright lead · Ken = the demonic grit · Thug = the character-voice duality (one rapper, many voices).

## III. RAGE: distorted, blown-out, loud

### 07 · BLOWN SPEAKER

`Uzi 25 · Ken 60 · Thug 15` · Tune: **Hard**

Bright ●●●○○ · Grit ●●●●● · Wet ●○○○○ · Width ●○○○○

**The sound:** Deliberately fried. The vocal sounds like it's tearing a cheap speaker: crunchy, clipped, jammed dead-center. Instead of floating over the distorted 808s, it lives inside them.

**Signature moves**
- Drive the vocal hot into a clipper. The clipping is the sound, not a mistake
- Parallel heavy-distortion bus, band-limited so the fuzz lives in the mids (no fizz, no mud)
- Compression after the distortion to glue the grit together
- Mono, with only a short dark room. No pretty tails

**Hybrid DNA:** Ken = the blown-out rage core · Uzi = a tuned melody that still reads through the fuzz · Thug = unhinged yelps that make the distortion bite harder.

**Best on:** Rage beats, distorted 808s, aggressive verses, mosh hooks.

### 08 · CHAOS WALL

`Uzi 20 · Ken 65 · Thug 15` · Tune: **Hard**

Bright ●●●○○ · Grit ●●●●● · Wet ●●●●○ · Width ●●●●●

**The sound:** Maximalist. The vocal becomes one brick in a giant wall of distorted synths: wide, washy, overwhelming. Organized chaos, *A Great Chaos*-style.

## IV. SLIME: elastic, expressive, lush

### 10 · SLIME WARBLE

`Uzi 25 · Ken 15 · Thug 60` · Tune: **Fast + humanized**

Bright ●●●○○ · Grit ●●○○○ · Wet ●●●○○ · Width ●●●○○

**The sound:** Elastic, wet and rubbery. The tune is fast enough to flip on every slide, yelp and crack (that addictive warble), while held notes keep their natural wobble. Warm, alive, unpredictable.

**Signature moves**
- Fast retune with humanize/flex: slides and yelps flip, sustains breathe

- Dynamic EQ on the squeal zone (upper mids) that only clamps down when yelps spike
- Stereo chorus/vibrato on a send and a medium lush plate

**Hybrid DNA:** Thug = warble, cracks, elasticity · Uzi = the melodic tune bed · Ken = thickness and saturation.

**Balanced kit (my pick):** 01 Cerberus (default) · 04 Neon Bleach (melodic singles) · 07 Blown Speaker (rage) · 10 Slime Warble (elastic flows) · 13 Vampire Haze (dark hooks). It hits all four boxes and each artist's corner.
